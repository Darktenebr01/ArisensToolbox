Dragon's Dogma 2 boss and monster summon tool

Summon a chosen boss or monster near an enemy group, or clear the enemies around you. The game still owns the spawn, registration and bookkeeping.

Download Arisen's Toolbox Install guide

How the summon works

The bridge hooks the game's own generate call. When the game presents a natural-spawn container, the container is restamped with the chosen species' base setup and variant, the player's ground position and prefab standby. The game's call then proceeds normally.

This is why the tool does not need a direct-spawn factory. A direct spawn was tested and crashed the game; it is not part of the release. The ride route keeps the game's own registration and cleanup.

Steps

  1. Open Mods and find the Boss tools card.
  2. Pick a boss or monster from the dropdown, or paste a custom code.
  3. Press Summon, then walk toward an enemy group. The next spawn becomes the chosen species at your feet.
  4. Use Identify nearby to log the codes around you, and Kill nearby enemies for a cleanup pass.
Medusa (ch255000) converts but can stand idle and event-locked. Some story bosses are not ordinary spawns. Test on a backed-up save.

Boss and monster roster

Boss tools cover 34 entries in the public release, including Evil Eye and the Dark Arisen DLC families. Later builds expand the monster roster from the game's catalog. Names come from the reference spawner data; codes without a source stay bare instead of being guessed.

Single-player only

Summoning changes the live game session. It is intended for offline, single-player play. Do not use it in online or co-op contexts.
